MEET THE ARTISTS

Re:Touch is a team of friends and BS Entertainment and Multimedia computing students from De La Salle Lipa! This website and interactive film serves as their final college thesis project, bringing together their passion for storytelling, technology, and multimedia arts. We aim to create an immersive experience that goes beyond the norm of traditional storytelling methods by allowing viewers to actively participate and influence the story and its characters.

Our team consists of four members in total, one Game Development major and three Digital Animation majors. Re:Touch aims is to create memorable experiences that share the stories of the creative industry that are dear to their hearts. Through our work, we hope to give these stories a platform and allow audiences to connect with them in a more personal and immersive way.
