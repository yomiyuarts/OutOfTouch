OUT OF TOUCH

SYNOPSIS

Out of Touch is a real-time interactive animation film about MC, an artist who has made it in the creative industry and succeeded in their dreams. However, they struggle to get up in the morning for a purpose, feeling lost as they try to search for the motivation. Enter into their psyche and go through their past, discover their story, make decisions, and help them realize their true feelings.

INTERACTIVE ANIMATION

Interactive animation is the graphic and visual changes that happen via user input whether through clicks, hovers, scrolls, or uploaded media. It is most recognizable in UI/UX design, but in our film it is those inputs that determine the experience of the viewer and how they change the story.
